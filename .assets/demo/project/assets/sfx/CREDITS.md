# Sound effects

`click-soft.mp3`, `key-press.mp3`, `pop.mp3` and `whoosh.mp3` come from the HyperFrames media-use bundled library,
sourced from [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) under the
[Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) (free for commercial use, no attribution required).
`whoosh-b.mp3` is `whoosh.mp3` pitched down 12% and low-passed at 6.5 kHz, so repeated cards do not reuse one take.

Music (v3): `assets/music/source-option8-rhodes-guitar-warm.mp3`, the owner-approved option 8 ("rhodes-guitar-warm"),
generated for this film with Google Lyria RealTime by `tools/lyria-structured.py --option 8 --seed 815` and fitted by
`tools/make-bed.sh` (cleanup, -16 LUFS, fades to silence at 56.8 s). `bed.flac` is that file decoded for the render.
