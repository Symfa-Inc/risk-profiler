# REVIEW: RiskProfiler demo

Delivered: `../demo-risk-profiler.mp4` (60.0 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 High yuv420p, AAC LC 48 kHz stereo,
10.3 MB) and `../poster.png` (t = 3.0 s, the hook poster at 82% FRAUD LIKELY).

## Creative calls
- Storyline A, "Two facts change the call" (see `../STORYLINES.md`); B ("the number first") and C ("two claims,
  one model") were not built.
- One continuous take inside the real dashboard. The only transition between chapters is a blur swap on the
  app's own #f8f9fb ground (app blurs out 8 px / 0.2 s, card words blur in, card out, app blurs back in with a
  1.03 -> 1 settle), used 5 times. Everything else is camera: 1.2x to 2.6x, power2.inOut, 0.95-1.3 s.
- No caption strip. Words on screen: the hook line, 4 cards (<= 5 words, DM Serif Display 116 px) and 9 callouts
  (IBM Plex Mono 500, 30 px) anchored with a leader and dot to the exact element, shown 1.4-2.2 s each.
- Typography: the film uses the app's two text faces, DM Serif Display (its h1) and IBM Plex Mono (its body).
  The rebuilt gauge keeps the app's own Rajdhani numeral, because that is the real component.
- UI = DOM rebuild of the running app: its compiled CSS (globals.css + Tailwind v4 layers, captured from the dev
  server, with CSS animations/transitions stripped so GSAP owns every change), its markup and class names, its
  fonts (the next/font woff2 files), and its own formatting functions re-applied to the recorded `/predict`
  answers. The native `<select>` menu is drawn in the app's popover styling (a headless capture cannot show the
  OS menu).
- Data: backend on :8341 (`uvicorn --port 8341`, `FRONTEND_ORIGINS` env), frontend on :3341 (`next dev -p 3341`).
  The frontend hard-codes `http://localhost:8000`, so Playwright re-routed those requests to :8341 in the browser;
  no code was edited. Both servers were stopped after capture.
- AI summary: `OPENAI_API_KEY` was present in the environment, so the summaries are real gpt-4o-mini output.
  Four runs of the 82% claim were made; SHAP (KernelExplainer, nsamples=100) varies between runs, so the run whose
  summary agrees with its own SHAP bars was used (`assets/data/predict-B.json`): it names age (+10.46%), annual
  income (+5.64%), four past claims (+19.76%) and no witness (+4.13%), all positive contributions in that run.
  Rejected: run 2 called the $5,000 payout a risk factor while its SHAP value was negative; run 4 invented "low
  income relative to the claim's estimated payout".
- Liberties, all inside the app's own design intent: the gauge sweeps with the app's declared transition curve
  (in the live app the ring mounts already filled, because the result remounts on every run) and the numerals
  count up; Feature Effects bars grow from the baseline as their rows stagger in with the app's waterfall-enter.

    the 57-word summary, the 26-word tooltip); every film line (cards, callouts) holds >= 1.4 s;

  the summary panel is 6 px shorter (column `li` gap). All cursor targets and callout anchors come from
  `assets/data/geom.json`.
- Sharpness (Laplacian variance of full 1920x1080 frames at zoom peaks): 9.0 s (2.2x) 289; 12.5 s (2.0x) 487;
  18.8 s (2.4x, open menu) 507; 22.5 s (2.3x) 400; 27.0 s 475; 33.6 s (2.6x, tooltip) 588; 36.5 s 474;

- Audio (rendered file): -16.1 LUFS integrated, LRA 2.1 LU, true peak -1.5 dBTP, no clipping, music from 0.03 s,
  silence 59.16-60.0 s. Measured, not heard.
- Music: Google Lyria RealTime, 3 candidates at 100 BPM, checked in spectrograms. m1 carried narrow tones at 8.8,
  9.1 and 14.4 kHz, m3 was dense broadband with 0.1 dBFS peaks; m2 (acoustic guitar, soft piano, shaker) was
  clean below 12 kHz, with tones at 14.8 and 19.2 kHz that the 12 kHz low-pass takes to about -95 dBFS. Bed starts
  at the take's section B (7.945 s), -3 dB at 110 Hz, fades 56.4-59.2 s. Clicks/key/pop: bundled Pixabay set,

## Fixed during review
- Result panel height snapped when the result mounted, so the form's bottom edge jumped during the pan ->
  the stack now grows to its measured height in 0.38 s with the entrance.
- Result framing left 30% empty ground on the right -> re-centred at 2.0x, callouts moved into that space.
- Feature Effects callouts sat over the form with long leaders -> anchored to the right end of each delta.
- Callouts shorter than their read time -> 1.4-2.2 s; summary chapter re-timed, pull-back moved to 48.4 s.
- Initial states set inside the paused timeline at 0 -> `gsap.set` outside it; second click of the double-click
  overlapped the first press tween -> moved 0.1 s later; two clicks on one audio track -> separate tracks.
- Prevented by construction (InsureCast lessons): no `will-change` on camera layers, `force3D: false`,
  `filter: none` after every blur-in, no unlayered `*` reset (the scaffold's was removed), all states live in
  the page and switch with fades, `immediateRender: false` on every tween.

## Weakest shot / with more time
- 40.8-48.4 s, the Executive Summary: the paragraph is 12 px app text, about 19 px on screen at 1.6x; it reads on
  a desktop but not on a phone, so the callouts and the highlight carry it.
- The button label crossfade (Evaluating -> Re-run) overlaps for 2 frames; a 0.08 s dip would be cleaner.
- With more time: a subtle motion blur on the camera pans, and a second take of the click sound.

3. Cropped panels: the cause was push-ins deeper than the panels (2.0-2.6x on 632x709 and 420x815 px cards).

## v4 (owner: picture looks soft) -> `../demo-risk-profiler-v4.mp4` (v1-v3 kept)

Same composition, edit and audio as v3; only the capture and encode changed. 57.6 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 High
yuv420p bt709, AAC 193 kbps (the render's own audio, copied), **7.3 MB** (~0.82 Mbps video).

**Root cause.** Not the CRF: v3 was already x264 CRF 15 (preset slow, deblock 1:1, aq 3); it is ~0.5 Mbps only
because flat UI frames are cheap. Measured against a lossless 1x snapshot, v3 frames score 40-41 dB PSNR, the same
ceiling yuv420p chroma allows. The softness comes from the capture: HyperFrames' mp4 path screenshots every frame
as **JPEG (q95) at device scale 1**, so 10-14 px UI text at 1.18-1.6x is rasterized once at final size and then
JPEG- and x264-compressed (grayscale AA, 4:2:0 chroma twice). There is no 1x-to-larger upscale and no scaling
filter in the path; the camera's 2D transforms keep text vector-painted (InsureCast lesson already applied).

**Fix** (`tools/render-delivery.sh`): `render --format png-sequence --resolution landscape-4k` (lossless PNG
frames at deviceScaleFactor 2, 3840x2160) -> ffmpeg lanczos downscale (accurate_rnd, full_chroma_int, bt709) ->
luma-only unsharp 5x5 0.5 in yuv444p -> yuv420p -> libx264 CRF 10, preset veryslow, tune stillimage, aq-mode 3,
maxrate 20 Mb/s. CRF 14 / 10 / 8 gave 5.4 / 6.9 / 7.7 MB with identical SSIM (0.9988-0.9996), so CRF is not the
limit; CRF 10 kept. The poster was re-taken from the 2x frame at 3.0 s.

**Verified (v3 -> v4):** text-edge strength (99.5th-percentile gradient) +11 to +29% at 12.5 / 22.4 / 27.0 /
36.8 / 42.0 s; noise on white unchanged (0.6-4.7); background colour identical (#f7fafb decoded); frame
alignment v3 vs v4 min PSNR 31.3 dB, no shifted frame; audio unchanged (-16.3 LUFS). 2x-zoom crops of the Feature
Effects rows (27 s) and the past-claims field (22.4 s) show darker, cleaner glyph edges and clearer red/green
deltas. Under 10 MB, so one file serves GitHub attachments too.
